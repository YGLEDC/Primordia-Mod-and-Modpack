# Primordia

**English** · [简体中文](#简体中文)

A systemic elemental fantasy mod for Minecraft **1.21.1** (Fabric), and a ready-to-play modpack built around it.

Awaken a Bloodline at an Altar, learn Skills of **Fire, Water, Wind, Earth and Space** from Skill Scrolls, shape them
with inflections and Disciplines, and carry them through four Custodians to the Reliquary at the End.

> Primordia is in pre-release (0.1.0). Bug reports and feedback are welcome in [Issues](../../issues).

## Install the modpack

The `.mrpack` holds Primordia and the pack's settings; your launcher downloads every other mod from its official
Modrinth page.

**Prism Launcher**
1. Click **Add Instance**, then **Import**, and choose `Primordia-0.1.0.mrpack`.
2. Click **OK** and wait for the downloads to finish.
3. Optional: under **Edit → Settings → Java**, give the game more memory (see below).
4. Launch.

**Modrinth App**
1. Click **+** (Create an instance), then **From file**, and choose `Primordia-0.1.0.mrpack`.
2. Wait for the install to finish, then press **Play**.

Other launchers that import Modrinth packs (`.mrpack`) work the same way.

**Memory:** the pack runs Distant Horizons and ships with the Photon shaderpack switched on. We suggest giving the game
at least **6 GB**, or **8 GB** with shaders. On a weaker PC, turn shaders off in **Options → Video Settings → Shader
Packs**.

**Language:** the game starts in English. Choose **简体中文** in **Options → Language**; I18n Update Mod then downloads
Chinese translations for the other mods.

## What Primordia adds

- **69 Skills across five Essences**, each taught by a Skill Scroll found in villages, structures, vaults and on
  creatures. Put Skills into eight Sockets and cast them anywhere.
- **The Awakening and the Bloodline.** Mine Primordial Crystal deep in the deepslate, raise an Altar of four tablets
  and awaken as a Primordian. Climb Grades D to A through the Kindling, the Tempering and the Ascension, and fit
  Augments in four Aspects: Kernels, Matrix, Cruor and Runes.
- **Inflections and Disciplines.** Each Socket's slots hold inflections that change how its Skill behaves.
  Disciplines, learned by practice, by trials against the Sundered and at a Custodian's defeat, grant them.
- **Reactions.** Elements leave Exposure; meet one with another for Steam, Wildfire, Molten, Mud, Mist or Dust.
- **Casting weapons.** Twelve Element weapons and the Resonance weapons, attuned and linked at the Conduit Table; four
  Essence swords and their Weapon Arts; the Four Essence Staff and the Lost Sword.
- **Bosses.** Bailan on her island, the Masked King in the Sky Archive, Cinderhorn in the Scorched Field and Kunyu in
  the Buried Archive. Their keys open the Ossuary beside an End city, where the Reliquary waits.
- **The Encyclopedia**, an in-game book in English and Simplified Chinese, given at the Awakening.

## Getting started

1. Find **Primordial Crystal Ore** between Y -64 and Y -54 and mine it with an iron pickaxe or better. Smelt the raw
   crystal in a Blast Furnace.
2. Craft a **Deconstruction Table** (two Copper Ingots, a Gold Ingot, four Deepslate Tiles) and make an **Artificial
   Primordium** on it from five crystals in a cross.
3. Put the table in the middle of a nine-by-nine floor with a three-wide, seven-high wall of one stone four blocks out
   on each side. Use the Primordium on each wall to carve its tablet.
4. Stand on the floor and throw the Primordium onto the table. The Encyclopedia explains the rest.

## Primordia 的内容

- **五大原质共六十九种技能**，每种由一张技能卷轴传授，可在村庄、建筑、宝库和生物身上找到。把技能放入八个技能槽，随处施放。
- **觉醒与血脉。**在深层板岩中开采原晶，搭建由四块碑组成的祭坛，觉醒为原初者。通过引燃、淬炼、登临三场仪式将阶级从 D 提升到 A，并在心核、形骸、灵血、血纹四种相位中装配增幅。
- **词缀与专精。**每个技能槽的词缀位可放入词缀，改变技能的表现。专精通过修习、对裂血者的试炼以及击败守护者习得，并授予词缀。
- **反应。**元素会留下附着；不同元素相遇，触发蒸汽、野火、熔甲、泥沼、迷雾或风沙。
- **法器。**十二件元素法器与共鸣法器，在通脉台调谐与连接；四柄原质之剑及其器技；四原杖与遗落之剑。
- **首领。**海岛上的白澜、高空书库中的覆面王、焦土中的烬角、深土典藏中的坤舆。它们的钥匙开启末地城旁的藏骸堂，圣骸在那里等候。
- **Primordia 百科全书**：觉醒时获得的游戏内图书，提供英文与简体中文。

## 新手入门

1. 在 Y -64 到 Y -54 之间寻找**原晶矿**，用铁镐或更好的镐开采，在高炉中冶炼粗原晶。
2. 合成**解构台**（两个铜锭、一个金锭、四个深板岩瓦），在上面用十字排列的五颗原晶制作**人造原基**。
3. 把解构台放在一块九乘九地面的中央，在四个方向距离四格处各砌一面三格宽、七格高、由同一种石头组成的石墙。对每面石墙使用人造原基，把它雕成碑。
4. 站在台面上，把人造原基扔到解构台上。之后的内容，百科全书会为你讲解。
